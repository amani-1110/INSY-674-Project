## Important audit findings

- The project already contains a substantive telemetry, tuning, ensemble, simulator benchmark and documentation workflow. Those experiments were reused and kept intact rather than duplicated or overwritten.
- There are 1,774 saved rows from three laps of one driver's single Monza qualifying session. Only laps 5 and 11 were used as training groups; their 1,181 rows do not constitute 1,181 independent examples.
- Lap 14 has been repeatedly inspected. It is not described as an untouched test.
- Original aligned CSVs use a transform fitted on lap 5. New cross-validation recalibrates from each fold's training-lap positions only; the exploratory EDA over existing aligned records retains the caveat that those files use the historic shared transform.
- The frozen Spa results are a valid recorded closed-loop simulator evaluation under their protocol. They are not an independent telemetry-prediction test.
- No complete Excellent-level compliance claim is made: the limited group count and absence of an untouched real-telemetry test remain material constraints.
