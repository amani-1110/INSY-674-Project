F1 driving: analysis and visual evidence

Verified simulator replays, two circuit maps, model-family comparisons and captions for the report. The planner sets corner speeds; the learned model proposes pedals. These are simulated observations, not real onboard footage.

Three measured driving stages

1. Without speed assistance

Original learned pedals; rule-based steering and pedal cleanup remain.

Monza: 91.8 s

INVALID - 13 departures

Silverstone: not tested

2. With assistance

Original learned pedals plus a rule-based speed planner and steering.

Monza: 205.4 s

VALID - 0 departures

Silverstone: 256.5 s

VALID - 0 departures

3. AI-learned steering + pedals

Expert imitation. No runtime speed planner, steering formula, launch rule or teacher fallback.

Monza: 209.1 s

VALID - 0 departures

Silverstone: 266.3 s

VALID - 0 departures

Measured result: learned pedals add 3.3 s (1.6%) on Monza and 1.8 s (0.7%) on Silverstone beyond the same planner. Both ablation drivers finish with zero departures. These are single-condition simulator tests, not broad generalization evidence.
